Refinement history

The global raw peak is retained with its element and integration-point ID in each result. It is not a qualified stress at an ideal clamp. The regular-region RMS does not replace it.

Boundary conditions

Acceptance policy

Final changes must be ≤2% in loaded-face displacement and ≤5% in regular-region RMS stress, with monotonic, decreasing increments. All force and moment residuals must be ≤0.1%, and geometry, coverage, warning and applicability screens must pass. Failed or oscillatory studies remain failed or incomplete.

Stress tensors are read at all four C3D10 integration points, rather than from averaged nodal contours. The fixed stress region spans x=55–75% of base length, y=20–80% of width, and the base thickness. A conforming internal partition preserves the same physical sampling volume across meshes without changing the solid. RMS uses volume-weighted four-point quadrature in affine tetrahedra. It does not assess every possible failure location.

Displacement is integrated over the loaded face using quadratic shape functions. Linear-displacement and elastic-strain screens do not establish material strength. Experimental validation and whole-part design approval remain outstanding.
